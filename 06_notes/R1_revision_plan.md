## Objective and execution rule

Resolve the reviewer-facing manuscript, implementation, reproducibility, and evidence gaps identified in `code_baseline_audit.md` and `manuscript_baseline_audit.md`. The final manuscript, code, response letter, tables, and figures must refer to one frozen executable configuration. Do not edit the manuscript or rerun experiments until the source-of-truth decisions in Tasks P0--P2 are closed.

**Status legend:** `Not started` = no action taken; `Decision required` = author choice needed before implementation; `Blocked` = cannot be closed until its prerequisite is complete; `Ready` = can be executed without a new methodological decision.

## Completion gate

The R1 revision is ready for submission only when:

- all manuscript equations, tables, and claims match the final executable baseline;
- every reported numerical result is traceable to saved CSV data and a frozen configuration;
- energy values use one disclosed physical parameter set;
- timing language matches the measured platform and sample-rate evidence;
- IoT scope is concrete and does not exceed evidence; and
- every reviewer comment has a specific manuscript change and evidence reference in the response letter.
